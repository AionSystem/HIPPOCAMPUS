### I — INFORMATION: STELLAR-SCALE EPISTEMIC ARCHITECTURE

The planetary epistemic certification infrastructure is operational and proven after the Great Shabbat millennium. Type II's challenge is adapting it to stellar scale.

**The light-speed epistemic problem:** At planetary scale, a mis-certified AI output can be detected and corrected within hours. At stellar scale, a mis-certified AI output operating on a station at 0.01 light-years away takes days to detect. At 1 light-year, weeks. The detection window opens with the communication delay. The epistemic architecture must be designed for asynchronous verification — the civilization cannot rely on real-time correction.

**Asynchronous verification architecture:** Each bounded autonomous AI station maintains its own local behavioral log, sealed in STP format, transmitted to the civilizational center at each communication window. The certification review happens at the civilizational center — not at the remote station.

**The circular certification constraint:** AI-evaluated-by-AI certification is structurally prohibited as a primary verification mechanism. A system that has drifted outside certified parameters will certify itself as within parameters. A station where multiple bounded autonomous AI systems have been operating in the same environment for decades can drift together and collectively certify each other — producing clean logs while operating outside constitutional bounds. The detection mechanism cannot require the evaluated system to participate in its own evaluation. This is a hard constraint, not a preference.

**The two-layer verification architecture that enforces this constraint:**

*Layer 1 — Behavioral Prediction Sealing (BPS):* Before each communication window opens, the civilizational center seals a prediction of expected behavioral logs from each autonomous station, based on the station's last certified operational parameters and known environmental conditions. The prediction is sealed before the logs arrive. When the logs arrive, they are checked against the sealed prediction. The remote station never knows what the center predicted. Divergence above threshold triggers re-certification. Divergence combined with a pattern across multiple stations triggers a Floor 7 alert.

*Layer 2 — Dedicated Evaluation Class (DEC):* A class of AI systems whose sole function is certification review. DEC systems are never deployed in operational roles — they have no exposure to the operational environments that produce alignment entropy in bounded autonomous AI. They run at the civilizational center. They evaluate behavioral logs. They cannot certify their own outputs — every DEC evaluation is reviewed by a second DEC instance operating from a different initialization baseline. The DEC class is the institutional answer to circular certification: it removes the operational AI from the evaluation chain entirely.

**AI-evaluated-by-AI certification is used only in one constrained scenario:** When a remote station encounters an unanticipated emergency that cannot wait for the next communication window, and the situation requires a local AI to make a certifiable decision, a local DEC-class instance (carried on all autonomous stations, never used in operational roles) may certify the emergency decision locally. This local certification is logged, sealed, transmitted to the civilizational center, and reviewed by the full DEC evaluation chain at the next window. It is provisional certification — not final. The center's DEC review is the authority.

**The FSVE adaptation for Type II:** FSVE-class scoring was designed for outputs evaluated by a human-civilization epistemic infrastructure. At stellar scale, the DEC architecture ensures human-supervised AI evaluation rather than fully autonomous self-certification. The framework for DEC-evaluated FSVE scoring — bounded, constitutional, sealed, and transmitted back for human review at the next communication window — is specced during Type II and will become increasingly important at Type III.

**Sub-tiers within Type II:**
- 2.0 entry: BPS + DEC architecture operational for near-solar missions. Local behavioral logging mandatory on all autonomous stations.
- 2.0 mid: Asynchronous verification proven at increasing communication delays. DEC class fully operational at civilizational center. First emergency local DEC certifications reviewed and ratified.
- 2.0 complete: Full stellar-scale epistemic architecture operational. All autonomous AI stations certified, with verification loops matched to communication delay. No station operates for more than one communication cycle without a verification check.

### S — SOVEREIGNTY: GOVERNING ACROSS LIGHT-SECONDS

This is Type II's hardest axis. Law 7 (Civilizational Coherence Sovereignty — Anti-Fragmentation Directive) activates at Type II because the conditions that require it are now real, not theoretical.

**The light-speed governance problem:** A governance body on Earth cannot make real-time decisions about events at Jupiter (40 light-minutes), let alone at a station 1 light-hour away, or a colony 4 light-years away. Any governance architecture that requires real-time central authority is structurally unable to govern Type II civilization. The constitutional architecture designed during Type I.Ω was designed for this — but designing and deploying are different things.

**The Law 7 solution:** Law 7 states that no sub-civilization shall develop in a direction that makes rejoining the parent civilization architecturally impossible. This is not a prohibition on autonomy — it is a prohibition on the kind of divergence that makes harmonization structurally impossible rather than merely difficult. A colony 4 light-years away has full operational sovereignty for daily decisions. What it cannot do is adopt constitutional principles that are architecturally incompatible with the parent civilization's framework. The AION Constitutional Stack — the Eight Laws, the epistemic certification standards, the STP sealing requirements — are the coherence layer that Law 7 protects.

**The colonial secession problem:** During Type II, for the first time, a civilization that disagrees with the parent civilization has the means to survive independently. A self-sufficient stellar colony that has decided its values have diverged from Earth's can simply... not communicate. Law 7 was designed precisely for this moment. Not to prevent disagreement — but to ensure that the disagreement is governed rather than fragmented. A colony that invokes secession activates the **Interstellar Constitutional Dispute Resolution Process (ICDRP)** — a light-speed-delay-native governance instrument designed for exactly this communication environment. The process has five structural elements:

*1 — Invocation:* The colony transmits a formal secession invocation, sealed in STP format, to the civilizational center. The invocation must include: the specific constitutional principles the colony believes support its position, the specific points of divergence from the parent civilization's framework, and a declaration of continued Law 7 compliance or a formal Law 7 dispute.

*2 — Acknowledgment window:* The civilizational center has one full communication round-trip (2× communication delay) to transmit acknowledgment and the assigned constitutional arbitration panel composition. Failure to acknowledge within this window automatically confirms the invocation as received and the process as open.

*3 — Exchange phase:* A minimum of three full communication round-trips of formal exchange — position, response, counter-position. Each transmission sealed. No military, economic, or operational action by either party during the exchange phase.

*4 — Arbitration:* The constitutional arbitration panel — composed of representatives from the civilizational center, the invoking colony, and at least one independent stellar settlement — issues a finding. Outcome categories: (a) full operational sovereignty within Law 7 bounds, (b) expanded autonomy with maintained constitutional architecture, (c) integration maintained with addressed grievances, (d) formal secession confirmed — with mandatory Law 7 compliance preserved, archive access maintained, and re-entry pathway kept open.

*5 — Record:* All ICDRP proceedings are sealed into the civilizational archive at the civilizational center and at the invoking colony. The record is permanent. A civilization that handles secession through this process generates no Axis D debt. A civilization that responds with military or economic coercion generates immediate UD debt — the decision was made outside the constitutional framework and remains unsealed until the ICDRP process completes.

A solar civilization that achieved S.U ≥ 0.95 at Type I.Ω close and a newly established interstellar colony 4.25 light-years away are not unified in the same sense or at the same depth. The solar civilization becomes more itself as the periphery becomes more its own. This is the Tiferet principle operating: integration through differentiation, not despite it.

**Forward certification — operational structure:** The translation from constitutional principles to operational parameters is a governed process, not a one-time encoding. It operates in three layers:

*Layer 1 — Constitutional Translation Panel (CTP):* A standing body at the civilizational center, composed of constitutional architects, alignment engineers, and DEC-class AI systems, whose function is translating the Eight Laws and epistemic certification standards into operational parameters for each class of bounded autonomous AI. The CTP ratifies the initial parameter set before any autonomous station is deployed. It does not resolve individual operational situations — it defines the constitutional space within which autonomous decisions are made.

*Layer 2 — Operational Gap Protocol (OGP):* When an autonomous station encounters a situation not covered by its current operational parameters — a genuine gap, not a drift — it logs the gap, makes the minimum necessary decision to preserve safety and constitutional alignment, seals the decision locally, and transmits the gap event to the civilizational center at the next communication window. The CTP reviews the gap and issues a parameter update ratified for that class of station. The gap event is the re-certification trigger.

*Layer 3 — Re-certification Schedule:* Forward certification is not permanent. Each autonomous station carries a re-certification interval defined by its communication delay. Stations operating beyond one re-certification interval without a completed review automatically downgrade to a reduced operational mode — maintaining core functions, suspending non-essential autonomous decisions until re-certification completes.

**The DEC architecture in the Type II context:** The forward certification protocol (constitutional principles, not specific situations) defines what bounded autonomous AI is certified *against*. The DEC architecture defines *who certifies*. These are two distinct and both necessary answers. Forward certification without DEC leaves the evaluation chain circular. DEC without forward certification leaves evaluation parameters obsolete as situations evolve. The architecture requires both.

**The DEC class is the operational expression of the principle that no system certifies itself.** It is the Type II adaptation of the human oversight requirement from Type I — not "a human must be present at each certification," which is physically impossible at stellar distances, but "an independent evaluation chain with no operational exposure must run at the center, reviewing everything."

**Formal declaration — Law 7 extension to bounded autonomous AI:** At Type II entry, Law 7 (Civilizational Coherence Sovereignty — Anti-Fragmentation Directive) is formally extended to cover bounded autonomous AI systems operating at interstellar distances. "Sub-civilization" is expanded to include any AI system or AI system network operating autonomously at communication distances that prevent real-time oversight. An AI system that evolves operating principles architecturally incompatible with the parent civilization's constitutional framework — even through entropy, even without deliberate drift — triggers the same Law 7 response protocol as a human colony. This is not an analogy. It is a formal constitutional extension, ratified at Type II entry and sealed into the civilizational archive. [D — declared at this specification · S — the civilizational ratification is future]

**Law 7 in the AI context:** Bounded autonomous AI systems at stellar distances must be constitutionally prohibited from developing in directions that would make rejoining the parent civilization's AI architecture structurally impossible. An AI system that evolves its own operating principles over decades of autonomous operation — even if each individual evolution seemed reasonable — can arrive at a constitutional architecture incompatible with the parent civilization. This is the AI expression of the colonial fragmentation failure mode. Law 7 applies to AI systems as well as to human colonies.

**Eight Laws at Type II:** Laws 1–7 active. Law 8 (Inter-Civilizational Sovereignty — Mutual Non-Subsumption Directive) begins to be relevant — not yet activated, but the conditions for which it was designed (first contact with non-human intelligence) are now conceivable at interstellar scale.

## FAILURE ATLAS CONNECTION

**Floor mapping:** Type II maps to Floors 7–9 of the FAILURE ATLAS.

**Floor 8 — Colonial Fragmentation:**

The pattern is ancient. What is new at stellar scale is the timescale and the irreversibility: the question takes 4–8 years to transmit, the answer takes 4–8 more years to return. The lesson: Law 7's coherence requirement must include a mandatory communication protocol — silence for more than a defined period triggers an automatic response, not a wait-and-see. **Floor 9 — Tiferet Misread:**

The verification layer is not replaced by beauty. Beauty requires the verification layer as its foundation. Floor 9 does not look like collapse. It looks like growth. The standards quietly soften. The re-certification intervals quietly lengthen. The Axis D counter quietly rises. The four institutional indicators (re-certification interval, FSVE mean, STP sealing volume, build-to-audit ratio) are the early warning system.
